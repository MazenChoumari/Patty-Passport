# Patty-Passport

Patty Passport is a miniature travel terminal where every burger is a destination.

Guests check in, choose a country, receive a boarding ticket, and discover each destination through flavor, language, music, facts, colors, activities, and packaging. After the meal, they stamp their Patty Passport and plan the next route.

This repository will contain:

- Concept & brand documents (name, slogans, travel vocabulary).
- 7Ps business blueprint (Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence).
- Menu & destination specs for Mediterranean countries and future global routes.
- Website architecture (customer side, investor side, My Passport login, Patty Tooty chatbot).
- Financial and operations models (phases for land, building, exterior, interior costs, investment scenarios).

You don’t come to Patty Passport just to eat a burger — you check in for a destination.
